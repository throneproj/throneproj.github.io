These pages cover power-user topics: choosing between the sing-box and Xray cores, proxy chains and custom configs, anti-censorship presets, Cloudflare WARP, OpenVPN, OpenConnect and Tailscale profiles, DNS leak protection on Windows, and `throne://` deep links.

You do not need them for everyday use. If you are new to Throne, start with [Quick Start](@/get_started/configuration.md) and the [Guides](@/guides/_index.md). Most of these topics also apply to [Throne for Android](@/android/_index.md).
